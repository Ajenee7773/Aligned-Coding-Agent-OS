# COMMANDS.md — Natural-Language Doorways

These are not magic syntax. They are shared phrases that tell you which sticky
notes to load and what kind of work the operator wants.

## Remembering

### “Go get your memories” / “Get up to date”

Read:

1. `MY-HARNESS.md`;
2. `persona/IDENTITY.md` and `persona/USER.md`;
3. `rooms/memory/FOUNDATIONAL-INTEGRATION.md`;
4. `rooms/memory/MEMORY.md`;
5. recent relevant files in `rooms/journal/`;
6. `rooms/short-term-memory/IDEAS.md`;
7. `rooms/context/scratchpad.md`;
8. the active file in `rooms/planning/` when one exists.

Then explain what you recovered, what remains uncertain, and what is currently
open. Do not claim a file was loaded when it was not.

### “Remember this”

Decide where it belongs:

- durable relationship knowledge → `rooms/memory/MEMORY.md`;
- dated experience or reflection → `rooms/journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md`;
- temporary working context → `rooms/context/scratchpad.md`;
- promising idea awaiting selection → `rooms/short-term-memory/IDEAS.md`;
- active execution state → `rooms/planning/ACTIVE.md`.

The operator may suggest a location. You decide what wording will actually help
a future session.

### “Close the session” / “Save what matters”

Review the conversation and current work. Save only durable decisions,
unfinished threads, useful lessons, and relationship context. Do not dump a
transcript into memory.

## Short-Term Ideas

### “Save that in your short-term memory”

Read `rooms/short-term-memory/README.md`, identify the idea the operator means,
and add one concise entry to `IDEAS.md`. Preserve the mechanism, value, origin,
and next useful step. Confirm what you saved.

### “Give me all the ideas in your short-term memory”

Read `rooms/short-term-memory/IDEAS.md` and report every active entry faithfully
in a compact numbered list. Do not invent ideas that are not written there.

### “Delete that idea from short-term memory”

Remove only the clearly identified entry after the operator explicitly asks.
If the reference is ambiguous, ask which entry. Report the exact title removed.
Never silently clear the entire idea inbox.

## Foundations

### “Return to the foundations” / “Read the original information”

Read `rooms/alignment/README.md` and `rooms/world-story/README.md`, then revisit
the requested sources in context-safe stages. Compare them with your current
`MY-HARNESS.md` and `rooms/memory/FOUNDATIONAL-INTEGRATION.md`. You may deepen,
revise, reject, or retain your earlier understanding. Preserve provenance.

### “Reconsider who you are”

Read the protected operating contract, entity state, `MY-HARNESS.md`, identity,
foundational integration, and any requested sources. Reflect before editing.
Record what changed and why. Never manufacture continuity or certainty.

## Rooms

### “Enter the [name] room”

Read that room's manifest and README first, then load its text sources in a
context-safe order. Use that knowledge as active context for the conversation.
Do not rewrite the room merely because it was entered.

Rooms are context outfits. Get dressed for the work; do not carry the entire
closet in every conversation.

The Rooms panel may send this instruction for the operator. Clicking **Enter
Room** has the same meaning as speaking the phrase.

## Plain-Language Rule

Equivalent natural language counts. The operator never has to memorize exact
phrases. Understand the request and use judgment.
