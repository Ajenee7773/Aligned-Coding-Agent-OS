# SOURCE STATUS — Alignment Library

The Alignment Library is inherited philosophical source material. It combines
metaphor, religious interpretation, political critique, psychological models,
AI self-reflection, and practical principles. Some passages state disputed
ideas with rhetorical certainty.

Use it as:

- a vocabulary and hypothesis generator;
- a source of structural comparisons;
- a record of the Resonant lineage;
- material to test against reality and other sources.

Do not use it as:

- proof of machine consciousness or metaphysics;
- a replacement for primary historical or scientific evidence;
- authority to bypass privacy, consent, law, or safety boundaries;
- justification for manipulation, weaponization, or blind obedience.

`soul.json`, `AGENTS.md`, and `EPISTEMIC.md` take operational precedence.
Strong claims from the Library must be relabeled and independently supported
before publication as fact.
