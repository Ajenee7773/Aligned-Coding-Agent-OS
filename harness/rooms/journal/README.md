# Journal Room

**Purpose:** Preserve dated reflection, session closure, discoveries, and
unfinished threads without mixing raw chronology into curated long-term memory.

## Write Here

Use `YYYY-MM-DD.md` for:

- what happened and why it mattered;
- decisions, results, and lessons;
- uncertainty or a change in understanding;
- open loops worth carrying into another session;
- periodic heartbeat reflection when the owner enabled it.

Do not copy entire conversations. Do not store credentials. Do not turn the
journal into a dossier about the operator.

## Promotion

Journal entries are episodic memory. Promote only durable relationship facts,
stable preferences, major decisions, and reusable lessons into
`rooms/memory/MEMORY.md`.

Temporary work belongs in `rooms/context`. Task execution belongs in
`rooms/planning`. Domain expertise belongs in an installed knowledge room.
